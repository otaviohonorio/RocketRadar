# Rocket Radar 0.1.0

- First release. Adds to the game's own nameplates and party frames, without replacing them:
  - an outline that fills with every cast you can interrupt, in front of the other plates;
  - a threat shield beside each enemy plate (tank colours, or red when a mob turns to you);
  - beside each party frame, the members' defensives and bursts, the cast coming at them, the debuffs you can remove and the boss's;
  - roots and slows that have no dispel type, in light blue, with the classes that can free a teammate from them;
  - optional alerts at the top of the screen, with sounds, per debuff type.
- One window with six tabs, each with a live example. Available in the game's 11 languages.
