# Rocket Radar 0.4.0

- New look: the window with a seal and title band, tabs on top, each tab's example on its own card and the Rocket family's buttons, dropdowns, checkboxes and sliders.
- Questions (such as reporting a problem) now open in the family's own window instead of the game's old dialog.
- Fixed in WoW Forever: the settings window no longer stops drawing halfway.
- Fixed: an open dropdown no longer lets the controls under it show through, and a button's or tab's text no longer disappears under the mouse.
