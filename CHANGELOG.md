# Rocket Radar 0.1.2

- Debuffs now really show on the right side of the party frames. Before, their icons were created with no size and stayed invisible.
- Each enemy casting at your group gets its own spot to the right of the frames (up to three), so two casts at the same player no longer hide each other.
- A cast whose target the game reveals a moment late is asked again, and a cast already drawn is no longer erased in the middle.
- The icons next to the party frames now stay under the world map and other windows; the nameplate and threat marks too.
- A talent that replaces a spell no longer shows the same cooldown twice on the left side.
