# Rocket Radar 0.5.0

- Threat, clearer: tanks see the shield green while they hold a mob, orange and blinking while they are losing it, and red with the name of whoever took it. Damage and healers in a group see only the dangers: yellow and blinking when they pass the tank's threat, red with "You" when a mob turns to them, and red with the name when another player takes a mob from the tank.
- Works with party frames from other UI addons: when the game's party frames are replaced, the cooldown icons and the debuff box attach to the new frames instead.
- The icons keep clear of art drawn outside those frames, like a round portrait on the side.
- Fixed Lua errors that could appear while looking for those frames.
