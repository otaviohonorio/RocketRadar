# Rocket Radar

<img src="Textures/Logo.png" width="96" height="96" alt="Rocket Radar">

Adds to the game's own nameplates and party frames, without replacing them: what to interrupt, who holds each mob,
what you can remove from your group, and your group's defensives and bursts.

An addon for World of Warcraft: Midnight.
